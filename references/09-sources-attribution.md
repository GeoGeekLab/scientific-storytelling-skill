## Method provenance

- **Course techniques:** target-journal analysis, the four genre-analysis questions, IMRAD mapping, section-specific components and writing tips, reverse outlining, ABT, loglines, and storyboarding. The storytelling module credits Randy Olson for ABT and the narrative spectrum and cites *Houston, We Have a Narrative* (2015).
- **Skill implementation:** task routing, worksheet fields, evidence markers, output contracts, diagnostic tables, and completion checks. These are implementation tools rather than original course instruments.
- **Original materials:** the course provides its own mapping template, ABT activities, and storyboarding resources. The Markdown worksheets in `assets/` are newly organized adaptations for this skill.

The university's teaching examples sometimes contain fictional scientific content. Use them to learn writing techniques rather than as research evidence.
