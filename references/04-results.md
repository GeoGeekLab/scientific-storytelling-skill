# Writing Research Articles — 4. Results

**Source:** https://writinginthesciences.uoguelph.ca/results/

## The reader's question

**What was found, and which patterns or trends were observed?**

## Three main components

1. **Tables and Figures:** Select evidence that directly addresses the research question. Include relevant contrary findings.
2. **Description of Your Data:** Describe important results in text and refer to their tables or figures. Focus attention without repeating every value.
3. **Identification of Patterns and Trends:** Explain observed similarities, differences, and trends. Report statistical results where the study performed them.

The handling of exclusions, withdrawals, and similar changes may appear in Methods or Results according to field conventions.

## Four writing techniques

1. **Choose visuals carefully.** Refer to each retained visual in the text. Use tables for precise numbers and figures for patterns or relationships.
2. **Label visuals effectively.** Provide informative labels and captions that help readers interpret figures and tables independently.
3. **Avoid repeating Methods.** Mention procedures only when needed to interpret a specific result.
4. **Repeat sentence patterns where useful.** Consistent phrasing makes comparable results easier to follow.

Reserve explanations of causes and implications for Discussion, unless the journal combines Results and Discussion.

## Completion criteria

Every reported finding traces to the supplied results. Every relevant figure or table is cited in the text. The Results clearly describe observations, including null or contradictory findings, before interpretation.
